手勢飛行 Gesture Flight

用 AI 手勢辨識撥動虛擬球，控制模擬器裡的四旋翼。
Swipe a virtual ball with your hand to fly a simulated quadcopter.

▶ 開始飛行 Start

進入後按右上角「手勢辨識」並允許使用相機。滑鼠 / 觸控撥球也可以用。
Tap “手勢辨識” (gesture recognition) at the top right and allow camera access. Mouse / touch swiping works too.

怎麼操作 How to fly

  • 左右手都可以，手放在鏡頭正前方 = 手在球上；用指尖撥球：前後、左右飛。
    Either hand works. Hand centered in front of the camera = hand on the ball; swipe with your fingertips to move.
  • 第一次使用請先舉右手，用來判斷畫面是否鏡像；之後若手的移動方向相反，按「鏡像」（M）。
    The first time, raise your right hand so the app can tell whether the camera image is mirrored. If motion ever feels reversed, press “鏡像” (Mirror, key M).
  • 手掌立起、面向螢幕扭轉 = 轉向（像轉方向盤）。
    Palm facing the screen, twist like a steering wheel = yaw.
  • 握拳 = 停；手移出色塊 = 球靠慣性繼續轉。
    Fist = stop. Move your hand out of the zone = the ball keeps spinning.
  • 手離鏡頭太遠（約 85 cm 以外）不會被採用，後方人群的手也不會干擾。
    Hands too far away (≈ 85 cm+) are ignored, so people behind you don’t interfere.

須知 Notes

  • 影像只在你的瀏覽器裡辨識，不會上傳。Video is processed locally and never uploaded.
  • 相機只能在 https 網頁使用；下載 HTML 直接開啟會被拒絕。The camera needs HTTPS; a downloaded file opened locally will be denied.
  • 手機請橫放並固定；第一次載入模型需要網路（約 8 MB）。On a phone, use landscape on a stand. The first load downloads the model (~8 MB).
